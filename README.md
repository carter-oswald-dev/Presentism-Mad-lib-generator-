# Presentism-Mad-lib-generator-

A simple, template-based Mad Lib generator to show the timeless fact that people in every time period think their's is the special unique time and place and that the end or start of something great or terrible is or will soon happen, whether or not it is warranted or not.

concept: 

The main idea behind this gag/joke project is that the ideals of people
thinking their in a special time period is that plenty of ideas around that often “rhyme” with each other through history, such as a society thinking that some fad or fledgling idea or object means the end of everything as we know it, or that it’s the peak of everything and things will either stay the same forever or get even better with an unwarranted and unrealistic amount of optimism.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
